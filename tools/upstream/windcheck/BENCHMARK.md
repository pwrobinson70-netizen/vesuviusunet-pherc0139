# Benchmark: auditor before / after `--windcheck`, folded phantom with planted defects

Generated by `score_on_phantoms.py` (seed 0, exact centre line, `phantom_scroll` v2 `folded` preset, turn 2 carries the S-shaped pleat). BEFORE = `trace_auditor` without
`--windcheck`; AFTER = with it. The last two columns are the two parts of windcheck on their own.

**What was tuned, so you can discount it.** The thresholds of `ct_aware_check` were set once while looking at turn 2 of the 128 phantom and its planted traces (crossing amplitudes
4, 8, 12, 16 voxels were tried; 4 and 8 are reported at size 128, doubled at 256). The block rule was added after the "cut" trace passed the global-fraction limit by 0.4 percentage points.
Nothing here is held-out data; the true traces are only 4 per size. The one auditor FAIL on a TRUE trace (turn 0 at 128, turn 1 at 256) is the existing switch_finder, not windcheck.
